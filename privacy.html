Privacy Policy

f8 — Film Camera · Effective 30 October 2026

f8 does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. f8 has no servers. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Camera and microphone

f8 uses the camera to show the viewfinder and take photos and videos, and the microphone to record sound with videos. The microphone is only on in video mode. Images and sound are processed on your device and never sent anywhere.

Photos

f8 asks only for permission to add photos to your library, to save your shots. It cannot read your library. When you import photos, you pick them in the system picker; f8 sees only the photos you choose, develops them on your device and saves a new copy. The copy keeps the original date and location, so it appears in the right place in Photos.

Location

f8 never asks for your location. Location already stored in a photo you import is kept in the saved copy and nowhere else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. f8 never sees your payment details or Apple ID. It only reads, on your device, whether your subscription is active. Family Sharing works the same way.

Crash reports

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer. This is controlled by you and governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

f8 collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
