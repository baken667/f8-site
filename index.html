Common questions

  • Restore my subscription. Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Share Pro with my family. f8 Pro works with Family Sharing for up to six people. Family members get Pro automatically; if not, they tap Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel or manage the subscription. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → f8 Pro.
  • Where are my photos? f8 has no private gallery. Every shot goes straight to the Photos app.
  • Open f8 from the Lock Screen. Add the f8 control in Control Center or on the Lock Screen, or set it for the Action button or Camera Control in Settings.
